# Stormhaven
A physics-based rogue-like where you buy upgrades and create shelter to protect humans from extreme weather events.

Made in 1 week for the ISU Winter 2025 Game Jam. Placed 1st out of 9 submissions.

## Solo project — full design, programming, and art including:
- Dynamic weather-event system that escalates difficulty over time
- Physics based building mechanics, used to shelter humans
- Shopping system, allowing you to upgrade your humans and materials
